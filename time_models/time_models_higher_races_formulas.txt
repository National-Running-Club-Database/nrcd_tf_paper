Cross-Event Time Models — 6 Races Per Season (Sprints & Distance)
===================================================================

Method:
  • Data: relay-inclusive outdoor CSVs, 2024–2026, results on or after March 1.
  • Population: athlete-seasons with exactly 6 valid results in the event group's
    discipline CSV (same counting as the race-volume analysis).
  • Athlete-season PB: fastest valid mark per event within that season (WA > 0).
  • Model selection: 5-fold cross-validation across 8+ candidate model families;
    the formula below is the lowest-CV-median-|error| winner for each pair.
  • 3000m Steeplechase excluded from distance.
  • Minimum n = 20 athlete-season pairs required to report a model.

Use:
  • Apply these formulas when the athlete has run exactly 6 races in the event
    group during the season. They are more accurate than the full-sample models
    for this population (mean CV median |error| 4.93s vs 6.77s across evaluated
    pairs).
  • Predict time in the unknown event from known PB(s), then convert predicted
    time to World Athletics points in the TARGET event table.
  • Treat CV median |error| as the typical ± uncertainty band (cross-validated).
  • Do not compare WA across events directly.

Coverage note:
  Only 10 of 24 event pairs met n ≥ 20 at exactly 6 races. All qualifying pairs
  are men's Sprints and men's Distance. No women's pairs qualified (insufficient
  athlete-seasons with 6 results and both events run).


Sprints — Men
-------------

100m -> 200m  (n=33 athlete-seasons, r=0.889)
  Model: robust_trimmed_linear
  Formula: 200m = 2.302 × 100m − 3.023
  CV median |error|: 0.363s | full-sample median |error|: 0.339s
  Ratio 200m/100m: median 2.041 (IQR 2.018–2.077)
  Median times in sample: 100m 11.91s, 200m 24.19s

200m -> 100m  (n=33, r=0.889)
  Model: ratio_linear (speed-dependent)
  Formula: 100m = 200m × (0.606 − 0.004753×200m)
  CV median |error|: 0.130s | full-sample median |error|: 0.126s
  Ratio 100m/200m: median 0.490 (IQR 0.482–0.496)
  Median times in sample: 200m 24.19s, 100m 11.91s

200m -> 400m  (n=22, r=0.582)
  Model: robust_trimmed_linear
  Formula: 400m = 0.941 + 2.210 × 200m
  CV median |error|: 1.261s | full-sample median |error|: 1.180s
  Ratio 400m/200m: median 2.253 (IQR 2.213–2.295)
  Median times in sample: 200m 24.07s, 400m 54.67s

400m -> 200m  (n=22, r=0.582)
  Model: ratio_linear (speed-dependent)
  Formula: 200m = 400m × (0.656 − 0.003884×400m)
  CV median |error|: 0.450s | full-sample median |error|: 0.350s
  Ratio 200m/400m: median 0.444 (IQR 0.433–0.451)
  Median times in sample: 400m 54.67s, 200m 24.07s

Not reported at 6 races (n < 20 athlete-season pairs):
  100m -> 400m, 400m -> 100m

Accuracy vs full-sample models (athlete-season baseline)
--------------------------------------------------------
These 6-race formulas beat the all-season athlete-season models on 9 of 10
reported pairs. Largest improvements:

  Pair              All-season CV   6-race CV   Improvement
  ----------------  -------------   ---------   -----------
  800m -> 5000m         23.74s        15.96s       −7.78s
  1500m -> 5000m        23.11s        16.19s       −6.92s
  5000m -> 800m          4.10s         1.88s       −2.22s
  5000m -> 1500m         5.82s         4.31s       −1.51s
  1500m -> 800m          2.99s         2.61s       −0.38s

The one pair that did not improve: 200m -> 400m (1.17s → 1.26s; n=22).


When to use these vs other formula sets
---------------------------------------
  • Athlete has exactly 6 season results in the group → use this file.
  • Athlete has exactly 5 season results → time_models_5_races_formulas.txt
  • Athlete has 5 or 6 results (combined cohort) → time_models_5_or_6_races_formulas.txt
  • Athlete has fewer than 5 results, or unknown race count →
    cross_event_time_models_report.txt or model_search/best_time_models.csv
