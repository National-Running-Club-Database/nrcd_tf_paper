New Factors Beyond Balanced/Specialized — Findings (5 or 6 Races Per Season)
=================================================================================

Question: Besides balanced vs specialized (wa_spread ≥ 50), what else
improves cross-event time models for 5 or 6 races athlete-seasons?

Helpful secondary factors:
  • pair_wa_gap_50: |WA(from)−WA(to)| ≥ 50 WA points
    joint>bal_spec 14/16 (mean Δ +1.664s); factor alone>bal_spec 15/16
  • pair_wa_gap_median: |WA(from)−WA(to)| relative to pair median
    joint>bal_spec 14/16 (mean Δ +1.262s); factor alone>bal_spec 12/16
  • from_stronger_wa: Source event has higher WA than target
    joint>bal_spec 12/16 (mean Δ +0.869s); factor alone>bal_spec 11/16
  • best_is_from: Athlete's best-WA event is the source event
    joint>bal_spec 11/16 (mean Δ +0.642s); factor alone>bal_spec 12/16
  • best_is_to: Athlete's best-WA event is the target event
    joint>bal_spec 11/16 (mean Δ +0.388s); factor alone>bal_spec 11/16
  • predict_to_best: Predicting toward the athlete's best-WA event
    joint>bal_spec 11/16 (mean Δ +0.388s); factor alone>bal_spec 11/16
  • best_event: Identity of best-WA event in the group
    joint>bal_spec 10/16 (mean Δ -0.097s); factor alone>bal_spec 8/16
  • events_competed: Distinct events raced in group: 2 vs 3+
    joint>bal_spec 7/14 (mean Δ -0.051s); factor alone>bal_spec 6/14
  • season_span: Days between first and last race: short vs long (median)
    joint>bal_spec 7/16 (mean Δ -0.159s); factor alone>bal_spec 4/16
  • wa_spread_tertile: Overall WA spread tertile (finer than bal/spec)
    joint>bal_spec 6/16 (mean Δ +0.611s); factor alone>bal_spec 8/16
