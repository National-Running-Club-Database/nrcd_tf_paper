New Factors Beyond Balanced/Specialized — Findings (6 Races Per Season)
============================================================================

Question: Besides balanced vs specialized (wa_spread ≥ 50), what else
improves cross-event time models for exactly 6 races athlete-seasons?

Helpful secondary factors:
  • best_is_from: Athlete's best-WA event is the source event
    joint>bal_spec 8/9 (mean Δ +0.764s); factor alone>bal_spec 7/9
  • best_event: Identity of best-WA event in the group
    joint>bal_spec 7/8 (mean Δ +0.786s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/8
  • events_competed: Distinct events raced in group: 2 vs 3+
    joint>bal_spec 6/8 (mean Δ +0.421s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/8
  • best_is_to: Athlete's best-WA event is the target event
    joint>bal_spec 6/9 (mean Δ +1.010s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/9
  • predict_to_best: Predicting toward the athlete's best-WA event
    joint>bal_spec 6/9 (mean Δ +1.010s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/9
  • pair_wa_gap_median: |WA(from)−WA(to)| relative to pair median
    joint>bal_spec 5/8 (mean Δ +1.445s); factor alone>bal_spec 7/8
  • from_stronger_wa: Source event has higher WA than target
    joint>bal_spec 6/10 (mean Δ +1.804s); factor alone>bal_spec 6/10
  • pair_wa_gap_50: |WA(from)−WA(to)| ≥ 50 WA points
    joint>bal_spec 6/10 (mean Δ +1.242s); factor alone>bal_spec 9/10
  • ability_tier: Max season WA in group: low vs high (median split)
    joint>bal_spec 4/8 (mean Δ +0.569s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/8
  • meet_volume: Unique meets in season: few vs many (median)
    joint>bal_spec 5/10 (mean Δ +0.337s); factor alone>bal_spec 6/10
  • nationals: Competed at nationals that season (yes/no)
    joint>bal_spec 4/10 (mean Δ +0.079s); factor alone>bal_spec 7/10
  • source_ability: Source-event WA low vs high (median)
    joint>bal_spec 3/8 (mean Δ +0.644s); factor alone>bal_spec 3/8
  • season_span: Days between first and last race: short vs long (median)
    joint>bal_spec 3/10 (mean Δ +0.396s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/10
  • season_year: Calendar year of the athlete-season
    joint>bal_spec 1/10 (mean Δ -0.007s); factor alone>bal_spec 5/10
  • wa_spread_tertile: Overall WA spread tertile (finer than bal/spec)
    joint>bal_spec 0/6 (mean Δ -0.031s); factor alone>bal_spec 4/6
