Cross-Event Time Models — 5 or 6 Races Per Season (Sprints & Distance)
===========================================================================

Method:
  • Data: relay-inclusive outdoor CSVs, 2024–2026, results on or after March 1.
  • Population: athlete-seasons with exactly 5 OR 6 valid results in the event group's discipline CSV (combined high-volume cohort).
  • Athlete-season PB: fastest valid mark per event within that season (WA > 0).
  • Model selection: 5-fold cross-validation across 8+ candidate model families;
    the formula below is the lowest-CV-median-|error| winner for each pair.
  • 3000m Steeplechase excluded from distance.
  • Minimum n = 20 athlete-season pairs required to report a model.

Use:
  • Apply these formulas when the athlete has run 5 or 6 races in the event group during the season (combined cohort). Mean CV median |error| across evaluated pairs is 4.46s vs 6.77s for all athlete-seasons.
  • Predict time in the unknown event from known PB(s), then convert predicted
    time to World Athletics points in the TARGET event table.
  • Treat CV median |error| as the typical ± uncertainty band (cross-validated).
  • Do not compare WA across events directly.

Coverage note:
  16 event pairs met n ≥ 20 in the combined 5-or-6-race cohort. Use when race count is 5 or 6 and you want the largest available sample for that high-volume group.

When to use these vs other formula sets
---------------------------------------
  • Athlete has 5 or 6 season results in the group → use this file (largest sample).
  • Athlete has exactly 5 results only → time_models_5_races_formulas.txt
  • Athlete has exactly 6 results only → time_models_higher_races_formulas.txt
  • Athlete has fewer than 5 results, or unknown race count →
    cross_event_time_models_report.txt or model_search/best_time_models.csv
