Specialization-Stratified Time Models (6 Races)
===============================================

Question: If specialization status is known, do cohort-specific formulas
beat the group-average 6-race models?

Population: exactly 6 races per athlete-season.
Specialized: wa_spread ≥ 50 WA. Balanced: wa_spread < 50.
Minimum n=12 to fit cohort model; n=20 to report pair.

Routed prediction: apply specialized formula to specialized athletes and
balanced formula to balanced athletes (with group-model fallback if cohort
train n < 12 in a CV fold).

Interpretation
--------------
Yes — cohort-specific formulas often beat applying the pooled 6-race model to the same specialization subgroup. The balanced cohort sees the largest gains.
Routed prediction shows modest gains; knowing specialization status helps most when applying the balanced-athlete formulas.
