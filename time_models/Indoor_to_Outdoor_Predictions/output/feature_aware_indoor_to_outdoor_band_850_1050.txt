Feature-Aware Indoor → Outdoor Time Models — WA Band 850-1050 (Width 200)
=========================================================================

Method:
  • Same indoor→outdoor pairs and feature routing as
    feature_aware_indoor_to_outdoor_findings.txt.
  • Band membership: indoor from-event season-best World Athletics score
    ∈ [850, 1050). The WA value is taken from the athlete's best
    (fastest / farthest) indoor mark in the from-event that season.
  • Pooled + feature cohort formulas; CV seed=42, folds=5; cohort n≥12;
    pair needs n≥20 after banding.

Use:
  1. Compute the athlete's indoor from-event season PB and its WA score.
  2. If that WA is in [850, 1050), use this band's formulas.
  3. Start from the pooled formula; switch to feature-cohort formulas when labeled.
  4. For pure forecasting (outdoor unknown), prefer bal_spec / best_is_from
     over from_stronger_wa / pair_wa_gap_50.

Indoor 60m -> Outdoor 100m  (n=46 athlete-seasons, r=0.478)
  Pooled model: median_ratio
  Formula: Outdoor 100m = 1.5702 × Indoor 60m
  CV median |error| (pooled routing): 0.169s
  Full-sample median |error|: 0.151s
  Ratio Outdoor 100m/Indoor 60m: median 1.570 (IQR 1.545–1.587)
  Median marks: Indoor 60m 7.08, Outdoor 100m 11.04

  Important features to know
  --------------------------
  • Recommended route: pair_wa_gap_50 (CV 0.066s, Δ +0.103s vs pooled).
  • How to determine it: If |WA(indoor from) − WA(outdoor to)| ≥ 50, use large_gap; else small_gap.
  • Other features that also beat pooled:
      - pair_wa_gap_50: CV 0.066s (Δ +0.103s) ← recommended
      - best_is_from: CV 0.090s (Δ +0.078s)

  How pair_wa_gap_50 alters the formula:
  • Baseline (pooled): Outdoor 100m = 1.5702 × Indoor 60m
  • If |WA(indoor from) − WA(outdoor to)| ≥ 50, use large_gap; else small_gap.
      [large_gap] n=24: Outdoor 100m = 1.5875 × Indoor 60m  (full-sample MedAE 0.091s)
      [small_gap] n=22: Outdoor 100m = 1.5509 × Indoor 60m  (full-sample MedAE 0.063s)

  How best_is_from alters the formula:
  • Baseline (pooled): Outdoor 100m = 1.5702 × Indoor 60m
  • Check whether the indoor from-event holds the athlete's highest WA among indoor-from + outdoor group PBs. Yes → best_is_from; no → best_not_from.
      [best_is_from] n=28: Outdoor 100m = 1.5830 × Indoor 60m  (full-sample MedAE 0.122s)
      [best_not_from] n=18: Outdoor 100m = 1.5423 × Indoor 60m  (full-sample MedAE 0.081s)
