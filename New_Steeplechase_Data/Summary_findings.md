# Summary Findings — new_steeplechase_data (Corrected Steeplechase Scoring)

**Data:** NIRCA club outdoor distance results, 2024–2026, `Relays_Distance_*` CSVs with
corrected 3000m Steeplechase World Athletics (WA) scoring.
**Full reports:** [DATA_NOTES.txt](DATA_NOTES.txt),
[Feature_Importance_Point_Band_Time_Models/steeple_band_gate_findings.txt](Feature_Importance_Point_Band_Time_Models/steeple_band_gate_findings.txt),
[coaches_analysis/README.txt](coaches_analysis/README.txt)

---

## 1. Bottom line

The original steeplechase scoring pipeline undercounted WA points — after the fix,
**steeplechase WA means roughly doubled**, which meaningfully raises steeplechase's
8th-place nationals threshold (from an "ultra-low bar" outlier to a threshold in line
with other field/distance events) while **preserving steeplechase's status as the best
relative nationals opportunity in distance** for both genders.

## 2. Data correction magnitude (per DATA_NOTES.txt)

| File | N | Legacy mean | Corrected mean | Legacy max | Corrected max |
|------|---|-------------|-----------------|------------|----------------|
| Men 2024 | 161 | 202.6 | **458.2** | 611.0 | 877.0 |
| Men 2025 | 100 | 230.9 | **492.1** | 611.0 | 878.0 |
| Men 2026 | 141 | 239.9 | **508.0** | 638.0 | 901.0 |
| Women 2024 | 78 | 344.2 | **595.1** | 606.0 | 834.0 |
| Women 2025 | 53 | 375.1 | **624.0** | 664.0 | 882.0 |
| Women 2026 | 65 | 361.4 | **612.4** | 673.0 | 889.0 |

All six files pass 100% gender-match validation after the fix.

## 3. Impact on nationals thresholds and clear-rates

| Metric | Before correction | After correction |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Men's steeple 8th-place bar | ~456 WA | **~740 WA** |
| Women's steeple 8th-place bar | ~511 WA | **~752 WA** |
| Men's steeple clear rate | 24/265 (9.1%) | 24/281 (8.5%) |
| Women's steeple clear rate | 22/135 (16.3%) | 23/135 (17.0%) |

**Interpretation:** steeplechase is no longer an "ultra-low bar" pathway (its threshold
moved from the lowest of all men's distance events to a level comparable with other
distance/field events), but **margin head-to-heads still strongly favor steeple** vs.
other distance events for both genders. Steeple remains the top distance focus for
clearing-capable rosters and points-maximization coaching frames. Women's scorer-count
distance priority now favors steeple over 1500m on conversion rate (17% clear rate vs.
8% for 1500m), with 1500m still the better depth play (larger field). Non-distance
(sprints/hurdles/jumps/throws) coaching recommendations are unchanged by this correction.

## 4. Point-band time model impact (feature importance with steeplechase)

Adding corrected steeplechase to the WA point bands increases the number of
athlete-seasons available for modeling in every band:

| Band | Athlete-seasons without steeple | With steeple | Gain |
|------|----------------------------------|--------------|------|
| 750–950 | 553 | **585** | +32 |
| 800–1000 | 350 | **368** | +18 |
| 850–1050 | 186 | **194** | +8 |

**Verdict from the gate check:** every band gains athlete-seasons, so feature-important
time models with steeplechase included were built (vs. only building them if no band
gained athletes).

Cohort-formula strategy mix (most-frequent winning strategy per band, out of 30/22/10
event pairs with feature cross-validation):

| Band | Pairs | Top strategy | 2nd |
|------|-------|---------------|-----|
| 750–950 | 30 | `best_is_from` (9 pairs) | `bal_x_best_event` (6) |
| 800–1000 | 22 | `bal_x_best_event` (6 pairs) | `from_stronger_wa` (4) |
| 850–1050 | 10 | `bal_x_best_event` (4 pairs) | `best_event` / `pair_wa_gap_50` (2 each) |

## 5. Limitations

1. **Retroactive correction** — this folder exists specifically because the original
   steeplechase scoring pipeline (used in `relays_findings/` and `coaches_analysis/`)
   undercounted WA points; any paper text or model built on the legacy steeple numbers
   should be reconciled against this corrected version.
2. **Distance-only scope** — this correction only touches distance/steeplechase; sprint,
   hurdle, jump, and throw analyses elsewhere in the repo are unaffected and unchanged.
3. **Small steeplechase fields** — even after correction, per-year steeplechase samples
   are modest (53–161 per file), so nationals thresholds and clear-rates for steeple
   carry more year-to-year noise than higher-participation events (see
   `non_relays_findings/Summary_findings.md` §3 for the general nationals-field-size caveat).
4. **Working copies vs. source** — `Distance_Relays_Findings/` here holds *working
   copies* regenerated by `prepare_distance_data.py`; always re-run the pipeline after
   replacing the source `Relays_Distance_*` CSVs rather than editing working copies directly.
