Indoor Track RQ1A — Best Event Summary (2024–2026)
=================================================

Question: For athletes competing in multiple events within a discipline,
which event corresponds to their highest World Athletics point value?

Method: overall best event + pairwise dual-event comparisons (same as outdoor RQ1A).
Data: indoor_analysis CSVs; all dates in files (no outdoor March 1 filter).
Relays: team WA points credited to each leg athlete.
Not run: RQ1B / RQ1C (no indoor Nationals).

Core individual events analyzed:
  Sprints: 60m, 200m, 400m
  Distance: 800m, Mile, 3000m, 5000m
  Hurdles: 55m Hurdles, 60m Hurdles
  Jumps: Long Jump, Triple Jump, High Jump
  Throws: Shot Put, Discus, Javelin Throw

Note: Each discipline folder includes shared indoor relay rows (4x200/4x400/etc.).
For specialization among multi-event athletes, use *_2plus_individual.txt and
pairwise *_individual.txt outputs (relay-inflated “all athletes” counts are less informative
for Hurdles / Jumps / Throws).

Per-discipline outputs: RQ1A_Best_Event/<Discipline>/


Headline findings (multi-event athletes / pairwise)
---------------------------------------------------

Sprints (athletes with ≥2 of 60m, 200m, 400m)
  Men: 60m is the modal best event (274), then 200m (181), then 400m (96).
  Women: 60m leads strongly (112), then 400m (50), then 200m (33).
  Pairwise Men: 60m beats 200m (319–207) and 400m (122–102); 200m beats 400m (171–128).
  Pairwise Women: 60m dominates vs 200m (161–37); 60m ≈ 400m (39–36); 400m beats 200m (89–49).

Distance (athletes with ≥2 of 800m, Mile, 3000m, 5000m)
  Men: Mile leads overall bests (242), then 800m (215), 3000m (194), 5000m (51).
  Women: Mile and 3000m tie for most bests (101 each), then 800m (94), 5000m (36).
  Pairwise Men: 800m beats Mile (260–205); Mile ≈ 3000m (192–189); 3000m beats 5000m (90–48).
  Pairwise Women: 800m slightly beats Mile (116–104); longer events win for dual athletes —
    3000m beats Mile (110–43) and 800m (34–20); 5000m beats Mile (30–16) and 800m (14–5).
    Gender asymmetry: women who double into longer events tend to score higher there.

Hurdles
  Almost entirely 60m Hurdles (55m is rare). Too few dual 55/60 athletes for meaningful
  individual pairwise specialization (Men n=2; Women n=0).

Jumps (athletes with ≥2 of LJ / TJ / HJ)
  Men: Long Jump 34, High Jump 24, Triple Jump 19.
  Women: Triple Jump 21, Long Jump 18, High Jump 14.
  Pairwise Men: LJ ≈ TJ; HJ edges LJ; HJ strongly beats TJ (12–3, n=15).
  Pairwise Women: TJ edges LJ; HJ edges LJ; TJ edges HJ (small n).

Throws
  Indoor throws are almost entirely Shot Put. Dual individual throwers are rare
  (Men n=5 Shot/Discus; Women n=2). Specialization RQ1A is not well identified indoors.
