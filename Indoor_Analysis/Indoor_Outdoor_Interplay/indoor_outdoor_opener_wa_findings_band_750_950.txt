Indoor vs Outdoor Season-Opener World Athletics Scores (outdoor opener WA in [750, 950))
========================================================================================

Question:
  For athletes with ≥2 indoor meets and ≥2 outdoor meets in the same year,
  do outdoor season openers start with higher WA scores than indoor season
  openers for similar events?
  Restricted to comparisons where the outdoor opener result is in WA
  band [750, 950).

Method:
  • Years: 2024, 2025, 2026 (indoor season file year paired with outdoor year).
  • Eligibility: ≥2 distinct meet_ids with individual (non-relay) results indoors
    AND ≥2 outdoors in that year.
  • Season opener: earliest meet by start_date for that athlete in the season.
  • Similar-event pairs compared only when the athlete contested the indoor
    event at the indoor opener meet AND the paired outdoor event at the
    outdoor opener meet.
  • Delta = outdoor_opener_WA − indoor_opener_WA.
  • Outdoor distance uses new_steeplechase_data (corrected WA) when available.
  • Relays excluded.
  • Band filter: keep a pair only if outdoor opener WA ∈ [750, 950).

Similar-event pairs:
  • 60m → 100m
  • 200m → 200m
  • 400m → 400m
  • 800m → 800m
  • Mile → 1500m
  • 1500m → 1500m
  • 3000m → 5000m
  • 5000m → 5000m
  • 60mH → 110mH [Men only]
  • 60mH → 100mH [Women only]
  • Long Jump → Long Jump
  • Triple Jump → Triple Jump
  • High Jump → High Jump
  • Shot Put → Shot Put

Eligibility / sample
--------------------
  2024: 74 eligible athletes; 85 opener pair comparisons (74 athletes with ≥1 comparable opener pair)
  2025: 50 eligible athletes; 58 opener pair comparisons (50 athletes with ≥1 comparable opener pair)
  2026: 60 eligible athletes; 75 opener pair comparisons (60 athletes with ≥1 comparable opener pair)
  Combined: 161 athletes contributing 218 opener pair comparisons

  n=218 athletes=161  indoorμ=789.3 outdoorμ=816.3  Δμ=+27.0 Δmed=+24.0  outdoor_higher=69.3%  indoor_higher=29.4%
  Verdict: YES — outdoor openers average higher WA than indoor openers for comparable events among dual-season athletes.

Caveats
-------
  • Only opener-meet results count; later-season form is ignored.
  • Athlete must run the paired events at BOTH openers — selection into
    comparable pairs is not random (e.g., many milers may open outdoor in 800).
  • Mile→1500m and 3000m→5000m are approximate cross-event WA comparisons.
  • 60m→100m compares different distances on the WA scale (by design).
  • Outdoor March 1+ filter is not applied beyond what the outdoor CSVs contain.
  • This file restricts to outdoor opener WA ∈ [750, 950); indoor opener
    WA is unrestricted (may fall outside the band).
