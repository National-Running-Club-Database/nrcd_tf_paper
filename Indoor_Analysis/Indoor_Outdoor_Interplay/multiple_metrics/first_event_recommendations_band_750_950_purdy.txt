Best First Event of Season — Athletes with Outdoor Opener in WA 750–950
Metric: Gardner–Purdy [scientific]
========================================================================

Question:
  For athletes whose first outdoor-season result has WA in [750, 950),
  which events tended to produce the highest Gardner–Purdy as the first indoor event,
  and which as the first outdoor event — by gender and event group?

Method:
  • Years 2024–2026; athlete-year must have ≥1 indoor and ≥1 outdoor
    individual (non-relay) result.
  • First result of a season = results on the chronologically earliest meet
    date; if multiple events that day, the highest-Gardner–Purdy result that day
    is used as the noted first-event performance.
  • Inclusion: outdoor first-event WA ∈ [750, 950)
    (same cohort definition as the World Athletics report).
  • Within each gender × event-group cell, rank events by mean first-event Gardner–Purdy.
  • “Best” recommendation within a cell uses events with n≥5 when available.
  • Sample: 428 athlete-years.

Caveats:
  • Conditioning on outdoor opener already in 750–950 truncates
    outdoor WA from below; outdoor event rankings are within that cohort.
  • Indoor first-event scores are unrestricted and often lower.
  • Same-day multi-event openers: highest-Gardner–Purdy mark that day is used.
  • Observational — does not prove causality of event choice.
  • Purdy/VDOT undefined for field events — those marks are skipped for
    first-event selection under those metrics.
